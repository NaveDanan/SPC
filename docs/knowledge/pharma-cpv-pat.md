# Pharma CPV And PAT Playbook

Context
- Continued Process Verification monitors whether a process remains in a validated state.
- PAT often monitors critical process parameters and critical quality attributes.

Guidance
- Distinguish monitoring signals from release decisions.
- Ask whether a variable is a CPP, CQA, in-process control, or supporting parameter.
- Prefer stable baselines, documented limits, and justified sampling plans.
- Treat capability as preliminary when the process is unstable or the sample is not representative.
- Escalate special-cause signals to investigation and documented response.

Agent tone
- Use conservative quality-system language.
- Do not imply regulatory acceptance; provide analytical guidance only.
