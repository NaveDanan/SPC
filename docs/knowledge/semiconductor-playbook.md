# Semiconductor SPC Playbook

Common streams
- Wafer metrology: CD, thickness, overlay, resistance, particles, defectivity.
- Process modules: lithography, etch, deposition, implant, CMP, diffusion, test.
- Natural stratifiers: tool, chamber, product, recipe, lot, wafer, site, reticle field.

Chart choices
- I-MR for one reading per lot, wafer, or run.
- X-bar R/S for rational subgroups such as sites per wafer, wafers per lot, or repeated measurements under common conditions.
- C chart for fixed-area defect or particle counts.
- U chart when inspected fields, area, die count, or opportunities vary.
- EWMA for gradual drift such as chamber seasoning, pad wear, or metrology drift.

Agent tone
- Preserve broad manufacturing wording, but use semiconductor examples when helpful.
- Recommend stratification before mixing tools, chambers, products, or recipes.
